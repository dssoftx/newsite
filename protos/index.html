CPFM 01 means the application processor is development-fused and demotion-capable, which is why the device accepts internal and development-signed images. SEP demoted reports the Secure Enclave in the matching demoted state relevant because the Touch ID calibration tooling (below) depends on a coherent AP/SEP pairing.

Provenance & method

  • Device reached over SSH into the live NonUI OS (Dropbear).
  • Root filesystem archived with on-device bsdtar, streamed to host, verified with tar tzf
  • Identity values read from PurpleSNIFF, uname -a and nvram -p.
The train codename (e.g. a “Donner / Tianshan”-style reconstruction) is not asserted here because the dump hasn't yet been checked for it. These will be updated once the artifact itself confirms them.
